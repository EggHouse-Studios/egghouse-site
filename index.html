03 / BEHIND THE STUDIO

EggHouse is run by Carey DiJulio, a Seattle-based product and business leader with 25 years in tech. Her path runs from the early days of streaming at RealNetworks, through a decade of leadership at Big Fish Games, to the CEO seat at Ripl — where she created Alkai, the world’s first AI social media assistant. She holds an M.S. in Information Management from the University of Washington and a B.S. in Behavioral Science from Drew University.
