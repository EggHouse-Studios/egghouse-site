02 / WHY “EGGHOUSE”

“In 2000, just out of college, a diviner in Côte d’Ivoire gave me an instruction: bury eggs in a swamp to ground my life ahead. Soon after I did my career started. Fifteen years later, I moved into a house near the spot — and EggHouse was born.”

— CAREY DIJULIO, FOUNDER

EggHouse runs on that same energy — grounding, future-divining, incubating. Hatch the idea. Give it time. Grow what works.

03 / BEHIND THE STUDIO

EggHouse is run by Carey DiJulio — a Seattle-based product and business leader with 25 years in tech, from the early days of streaming at RealNetworks, to a decade of leadership at Big Fish Games, to CEO role at Ripl where she created Alkai - the world's first AI social media assistant.  She holds an M.S. in Information Management, University of Washington and a B.S. in Behavioral Science from Drew University.
